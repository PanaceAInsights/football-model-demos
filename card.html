B1

Counter attack

B1.0 · B1.2 · B5.2
We have just won the ball.
  • Win it and play a simple, positive first pass to start the fast break.
  • Get the ball out of the regain area at once.
  • Keep your place in the 2-3 rest defence in front of the centre-backs.
C1

Counter press

C1 · C1.1a
We have just lost the ball.
  • Before we lose it, stand where no long ball can go over your head.
  • 3 or more of us near the ball: press, and win it back inside 6 seconds.
  • Fewer than 3: stop or delay their counter attack and get into our 4-4-2.
B2

Build up

B2a.2
Our ball at the back; they are organised.
  • Make a diagonal shape with the goalkeeper and centre-backs.
  • Scan for pressing runs and know your next pass before it arrives.
  • Drop between the centre-backs to make the extra player against a press.
C2

Defensive shape

C2a.3 · C2a.4 · C2b.5
Their ball; we are set behind it.
  • Screen the space in front of the centre-backs.
  • From their goal kicks, mark their #8 or #10 to keep them off the ball.
  • Below the top of the D, one of you drops into the back line: 5-4-1.
B3

Create and finish

B3.1 · B3.2 · B3.5
We are in their third.
  • One of you goes box to box and joins the six attacking players.
  • Help switch play: central players are the key to it.
  • The other holds the rest defence: delay, deny forward passes, win it back.
C3

Defend the box

C3.1 · C3.4
They are in and around our box.
  • On crosses, cover the penalty spot and track late runs from midfield.
  • Block shots from the edge of the box.
  • Win the second ball and move it out in one or two touches.
